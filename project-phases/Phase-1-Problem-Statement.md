Phase 1 — Brainstorming & Ideation

Project Name: FitBuddy – AI-Based Personalized Fitness Assistant

Problem Idea:
Users often need personalized workout guidance based on their individual details and fitness goals. A generic workout plan may not match every user's needs.

Proposed Idea:
FitBuddy is an AI-based fitness assistant that generates personalized 7-day workout plans and provides concise nutrition guidance based on user information.

Main Objectives:

Generate personalized 7-day workout plans.
Provide concise nutrition tips.
Update workout plans based on user feedback.
Provide an interactive AI-based experience.

Target Users:
People looking for personalized fitness guidance.
